# Story Design Phase

## Goal

Lock the creative blueprint before drafting starts.

## Required artifacts

- `story-bible`
- `format-design-spec`

## Include

- premise and logline
- fair-play mystery clue map
- continuity ledger
- setting rules
- cast arcs
- plot architecture
- target length range
- draft readiness criteria

## Must not do

- Do not draft manuscript scenes.
- Do not mutate ReadersBase.
- Do not create Publishing work.

## Exit gate

CEO approves story lock before Draft issues are created.
