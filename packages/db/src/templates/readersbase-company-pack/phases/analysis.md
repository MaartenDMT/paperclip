# Analysis Phase

## Goal

Convert approved research into a production thesis, format fit, genre-reader promise, and bridge-safe metadata.

## Required artifacts

- `classification-bridge-manifest`
- `genre-reader-promise-gate`

## Include

- selected format and genre/subgenre keys
- reader promise and shelf fit
- taxonomy source references
- rejected alternatives
- scope and budget risks
- no-live-mutation confirmation

## Must not do

- Do not mutate ReadersBase.
- Do not draft story scenes.
- Do not create Draft work.

## Exit gate

Production, Genre, and Format leads agree the pilot is worth designing and bridge metadata remains metadata-only.
