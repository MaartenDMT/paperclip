# Research Phase

## Goal

Create the evidence base before story decisions expand canon.

## Required artifact

- `research-brief`

## Include

- comparable works
- reader promise evidence
- ReadersBase taxonomy source refs
- setting and domain notes
- confidence labels
- risks
- open questions

## Must not do

- Do not draft story scenes.
- Do not mutate ReadersBase.
- Do not publish externally.
- Do not start mass generation.

## Exit gate

Research lead and Genre lead accept the research brief before Analysis starts.
